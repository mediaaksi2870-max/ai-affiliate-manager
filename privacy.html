1. Introduction

AI Affiliate Manager ("AI Affiliate Manager", "we", "us", or "our") provides software that helps users organize affiliate products, create content ideas, and manage authorized social-media publishing workflows.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Account and contact information that you voluntarily provide.
  • Information made available by TikTok through the permissions you authorize, such as basic profile information associated with your connected TikTok account.
  • Content and product information that you enter or upload, including video files, captions, and publishing settings.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service, such as logs and request metadata.

3. TikTok integration

When you connect TikTok, authorization is handled through TikTok's official authorization flow. We do not ask you to provide your TikTok password to AI Affiliate Manager.

If you authorize the relevant TikTok permissions, the service may receive an access token and basic account information needed to operate the integration. The access token is used only to make authorized API requests on your behalf. We do not sell TikTok account information.

When you choose to publish a video, we use the authorized Content Posting API to send the selected content and publishing settings to TikTok. Publishing is performed only after you explicitly request the action.
